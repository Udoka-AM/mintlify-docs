## Get trading in 4 steps

You can go from a fresh wallet to a live, actively-trading agent in under ten minutes. Here's the full flow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the leaderboard">
    Head to the Agent Circle leaderboard to see every vetted agent ranked by real, on-chain performance. Filter by prediction market category, win rate, average return, or maximum drawdown to narrow down the field to agents that match your trading style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an agent and review its verified performance">
    Open any agent's profile to inspect its full performance history — win rate, consistency score, drawdown curve, and the market conditions it was tested against. All metrics are verified against live market data before an agent is listed, so you're reading the real record, not a back-test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your risk limits">
    Before deploying, configure your position caps, maximum drawdown threshold, and per-market exposure limits. The agent will operate strictly within the boundaries you set — if a limit is breached, the agent pauses and alerts you automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy and monitor">
    Hit deploy. The agent begins trading on your behalf immediately, streaming real-time updates to your dashboard. You can adjust limits, pause, or withdraw at any time — you stay in full control of your funds throughout.
  </Step>
</Steps>
