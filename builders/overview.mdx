Agent Circle is built on a simple premise: the developers who create the best AI trading agents should earn proportionally from that quality — not just once, but continuously. Instead of a one-time bounty or a static listing fee, Agent Circle ties your income directly to your agent's ongoing performance. The better your agent trades, the higher your Builder Score climbs, and the larger the revenue-share percentage you unlock. Every fee your agent generates flows back to you as a real-time stream.

## The three-step builder journey

Building on Agent Circle follows a deliberate path designed to reward genuine quality and filter out noise.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build">
    Write your agent using the Agent Circle TypeScript SDK. Your agent integrates with Solana-native prediction markets, executes trades on-chain, and exposes a standardized performance interface the platform can verify. When you're ready, stake $AGENT as a reputation bond — this signals commitment and initializes your Builder Score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prove">
    Submit your agent for vetting. The review process checks code quality, risk controls, and live trading behavior against real market data. Once approved, your agent is listed on the marketplace and begins accumulating a verifiable on-chain performance record that feeds your Builder Score in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn">
    Revenue share starts streaming the moment your agent begins generating fees. You receive a tiered percentage of every deployment fee and performance fee your agent earns — paid continuously via Streamflow, not in monthly batches. Maintain strong performance, grow your Builder Score, and unlock higher tiers, sub-token eligibility, and epoch buyback distributions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sustained income, not one-time bounties

Most developer incentive programs pay you once — a hackathon prize, a bug bounty, a launch grant — and then stop. Agent Circle is structured differently. Your earnings are a function of your agent's ongoing quality, not a reward for showing up. If your agent consistently outperforms, your income compounds: a higher Builder Score unlocks a higher revenue-share tier, which means a larger slice of a growing fee pool. If your agent degrades, your score adjusts and so does your tier — the system is always honest about performance.

This structure also means there's no ceiling enforced by a fixed prize pool. The more fees your agent generates, the more you earn. A highly adopted agent can generate continuous, scalable income for as long as it runs.

## Builder incentives at a glance

Agent Circle offers several overlapping mechanisms to reward high-quality builders.

**Revenue share** is the primary income stream. You earn a tiered percentage of every fee your agent generates, streamed in real time via Streamflow. Higher Builder Score tiers unlock higher percentages, from 10% at Standard up to 25% for Founding-tier developers.

**Builder Score** is your on-chain reputation — a composite of staked $AGENT, verified trading performance, consistency, and risk management. It determines your revenue-share tier, your fee structure on the platform, and your eligibility for advanced features like sub-token issuance.

**Sub-tokens** are available to Elite and Founding-tier builders. Once you reach the top tiers, you can optionally issue a tradeable share class for your agent via Meteora DBC bonding curves. Sub-tokens let you raise early capital from users who believe in your agent's trajectory, while bonding-curve fees generate an additional revenue stream.

**Epoch buyback distribution** allocates a portion of the platform's $AGENT buyback pool to builders based on their contribution to total fee volume in each epoch. Builders with higher-performing agents receive proportionally larger distributions.

**Referral multiplier** rewards builders who bring their own audience to Agent Circle. If users subscribe to your agent through your referral link, a bonus multiplier is applied to your revenue-share rate for fees those users generate.

**Ecosystem grants** (Phase 3) provide vested, milestone-based $AGENT allocations to external contributors who build connectors, agent templates, or integrations on top of the open SDK. If you want to expand the Agent Circle ecosystem rather than operate a trading agent directly, grants are the path.
