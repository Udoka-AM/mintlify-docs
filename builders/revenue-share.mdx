When users deploy or subscribe to your agent on Agent Circle, they pay fees — deployment fees when they initialize a position and performance fees when your agent generates returns. A percentage of every one of those fees flows directly back to you. That percentage is not fixed: it scales with your Builder Score, which is itself a function of your agent's verified trading record. The better your agent performs over time, the higher your score climbs, and the more of each fee dollar you keep.

## How revenue share is calculated

Your revenue share is applied to two fee types your agent generates:

- **Deployment fees** — charged to users each time they deploy capital to your agent. A flat percentage of this fee is directed to your revenue-share pool.
- **Performance fees** — charged as a percentage of profits your agent generates for users. The platform collects this fee at settlement, and your revenue-share tier determines what portion you receive.

The calculation is straightforward: multiply the total fees your agent generates in a given period by your revenue-share rate. Your rate is determined by your Builder Score tier at the time the fee is collected — if your tier changes mid-period, the new rate applies from the point of change forward.

There is no cap on total earnings. An agent that generates high fee volume simply produces proportionally higher builder income.

## Streaming mechanics via Streamflow

Agent Circle does not batch your earnings and pay them out monthly. Revenue is streamed continuously via Streamflow, a Solana-native token streaming protocol. As your agent generates fees, your claimable balance updates in near real time. You can withdraw accumulated earnings at any time from the builder dashboard — there is no lockup on streamed revenue.

Streaming is tied to trailing performance rather than a fixed schedule. Your Streamflow stream adjusts based on a rolling performance window: if your agent's recent performance drops, the stream rate reflects that. If performance recovers, the stream rate adjusts upward. This means your payout always reflects what your agent is actually doing right now, not what it did three months ago.

## Revenue-share tiers

Your Builder Score places you in one of four tiers. Higher tiers unlock a greater percentage of fees generated by your agent.

| Tier | Builder Score Range | Revenue-Share Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 0–50 | 10% |
| Verified | 51–75 | 15% |
| Elite | 76–90 | 20% |
| Founding | 91–100 | 25% |

Tier assignments are recalculated continuously as your Builder Score updates. There is no waiting period to move up — a sustained improvement in agent performance will move you into the next tier as soon as your score crosses the threshold. Likewise, a decline in performance can move you down.

Founding-tier status is available to developers accepted into the Phase 0 founding cohort and to builders who achieve and sustain top-tier Builder Score metrics after the founding cohort closes.

## Viewing your earnings in the dashboard

Your builder dashboard provides a real-time view of your revenue-share activity:

- **Claimable balance** — the total streamed earnings available for immediate withdrawal
- **Stream rate** — your current earnings per hour based on recent agent performance and your active tier
- **Fee breakdown** — a split of deployment fees vs. performance fees driving your revenue
- **Tier status** — your current Builder Score tier and the threshold to the next tier
- **Historical payouts** — a ledger of all past withdrawals with on-chain transaction references

To claim earnings, navigate to **Dashboard → Earnings → Withdraw** and confirm the transaction in your connected Solana wallet. Withdrawals settle on-chain via the Streamflow contract and are final.

## Referral and distribution multiplier

If you bring your own audience to Agent Circle — users who subscribe to your agent through a referral link you generate — a multiplier is applied on top of your base revenue-share rate for fees those users generate.

The multiplier rewards builders who actively grow the platform's user base. It stacks with your tier rate: a Verified-tier builder at 15% with an active referral multiplier of 1.2× earns an effective 18% rate on referred-user fees. The multiplier is computed per user and applied for the duration of that user's activity on the platform through your referral.

You can generate and track referral links from **Dashboard → Referrals**. The dashboard shows referred user count, attributed fee volume, and the resulting multiplier applied to your current stream.

<Warning>
A portion of your revenue share is denominated in $AGENT, the platform's native token. The value of $AGENT fluctuates with market conditions. While the fee percentage you earn is fixed by your tier, the USD-equivalent value of token-denominated payouts will vary. Plan your finances accordingly and do not treat projected $AGENT earnings as a fixed-dollar income.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Builder Score is compounded by consistency, not just peak performance. An agent that delivers steady, moderate returns over many weeks will accumulate a higher score than one that spikes once and then goes quiet. Prioritize robust risk controls and consistent execution — small improvements to drawdown management often have an outsized positive effect on your trailing score, which in turn lifts your tier and revenue-share rate.
</Tip>
